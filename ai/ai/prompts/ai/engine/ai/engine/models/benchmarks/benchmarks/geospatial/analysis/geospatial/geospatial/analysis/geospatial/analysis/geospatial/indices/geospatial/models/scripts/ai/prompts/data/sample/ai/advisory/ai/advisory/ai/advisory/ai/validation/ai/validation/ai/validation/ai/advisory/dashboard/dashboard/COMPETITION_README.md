# 🛰️ LIVA GeoSense AI

## Satellite-Powered Geospatial Intelligence for Smarter Field Decisions

LIVA GeoSense AI is a geospatial intelligence platform designed to transform satellite observations into actionable field intelligence.

The platform monitors vegetation conditions, detects changes, identifies potential stress zones, generates recommendations and converts those insights into operational field tasks.

---

# 🌍 The Problem

Agricultural and environmental teams often have access to large amounts of satellite and field data, but turning that information into a clear operational decision can be difficult.

Traditional monitoring can involve:

- Manual field inspections
- Delayed detection of vegetation stress
- Fragmented data
- Reactive decision-making
- Difficulty prioritising field interventions

LIVA GeoSense addresses this gap by creating a continuous intelligence workflow.
