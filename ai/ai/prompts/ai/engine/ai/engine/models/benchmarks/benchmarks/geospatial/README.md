# LIVA GeoSense Geospatial Intelligence

The Geospatial Intelligence layer provides the spatial context
used by LIVA GeoSense AI.

## Purpose

The system combines geospatial information with local AI to
provide agricultural intelligence.

## Core Data

The MVP can work with:

- Latitude
- Longitude
- Farm area
- NDVI
- Vegetation condition
- Vegetation change
- Rainfall
- Soil information
- Land-use information
- Environmental risk indicators

## Planned Analysis

### Vegetation Health

Analyse vegetation indices such as NDVI to identify areas
that may require attention.

### Spatial Comparison

Compare different areas within the same farm or between farms.

### Change Detection

Identify changes in vegetation condition over time.

### Risk Assessment

Combine available geospatial indicators to identify possible
agricultural risks.

## Important Limitation

Geospatial indicators should not be treated as definitive
diagnoses.

For example, a decrease in NDVI may indicate vegetation
stress, but additional information may be required to
determine the exact cause.
